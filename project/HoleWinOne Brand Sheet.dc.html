06 Do & Don't
Do
  • Let the gold WIN pop against deep green.
  • Keep it premium-leisure — at home next to a winery.
  • Lead with the jackpot and the dare.
  • Use the coin-target mark small and standalone.
Don't
  • Use flags, tees or clubs as the hero.
  • Add crests, serifs or "established 1920" airs.
  • Go rainbow or cheap-fairground bright.
  • Imply golf skill over "have a go & win".
