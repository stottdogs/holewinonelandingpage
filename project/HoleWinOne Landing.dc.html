Why it's legit

Real money. Verified shots.

No honour system, no catch. The course's hardware does the judging.

Every shot is watched

Each Par 3 has permanent on-course sensors and a camera that confirm an ace the moment it drops. No disputes.

Jackpots are real

The pool grows $1 with every attempt — floor of $2,500, cap of $5,000 AUD. What you see ticking is what you win.

Fault = auto-refund

If the hardware ever hiccups on your attempt, your $5 is refunded automatically. You're never charged for a shot we can't verify.
